# Market language — verbatim

From Leon's buyer research (Trustpilot, Spotify community forums, real artists).
These are the market's own words. Use them in ads, not paraphrases.

## The three questions that decide the sale (price is NOT one of them)
1. "Are these real listeners or bots?"
2. "Does the growth stay when I stop paying?"
3. "Does this provider actually know my genre?"

## Verbatim quotes
- "Are they a scam?" — the most common first question about any unknown provider.
- "It is always a gamble when trusting someone new to promote your music."
- "I am worried that streamify is just that... bots."
- "will these be real listeners or bots?"
- "First day after signing up I got around 1200 listeners... Next day, zero...
  It's not organic, not genuine listeners who like your music."
- "The suspicious biweekly spikes... magically disappeared the moment my
  campaign stopped... hardly anyone is listening anymore."
- "streams dramatically dropped... the exact opposite of what they advertise."
- "You will get plays... but you will get little to no engagement."
- "my distribution company flagged my streams as artificial bot traffic...
  they've damaged my credibility."
- "song got removed from Spotify 3 weeks later for inauthentic streams."
- "I asked for that [report] weeks ago and they told me they would provide it
  soon. I haven't received anything."
- "Every single one of the positive reviews is a paid for scam."
- "Ran it through the bot checker and completely clean... I highly recommend
  doing this."
- "at least you know that a human being made that decision."
- "No guarantees of playlist placements and no guarantees of streams. This is
  how you know the streams you get are for real."
- "Is it worth it?" — the dominant search phrase for every provider.

## The deepest fear
Not lost money — **lost music.** The release gets flagged and removed, and the
profile carries it for years. "Cheap promotion is the most expensive thing in
this business."

## Red flags the market actively looks for
Guaranteed stream numbers · prices below market · playlists with generic names
and bot followers · no nameable people behind the company · stolen team photos ·
generic five-star reviews.

## What convinces
Real Spotify-for-Artists screenshots with follower/save curves over time ·
retention metrics (save rate, streams-per-listener, cost-per-follower) rather
than stream counts · named testimonials with a real handle · transparency about
what is NOT guaranteed · a human being reachable.

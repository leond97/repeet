# How Leon writes (from 15 Kit broadcasts)

repeet copy uses "we" (agency), but the rhythm and honesty below are Leon's and
should carry into every ad.

- Short paragraphs, one idea each. Plain words.
- Writes `&` instead of "and" constantly.
- Concrete numbers, never vague claims: "$297. 5 spots", "40k streams",
  "50 days", "3 demos".
- Names real people as proof: Niclas, Aron, Armen, Silvio, Galen.
- Admits things openly: "I'm talking into a void & that's on me, not you."
- Refuses money: "If nothing is close, I'll say so & I won't take your money."
- Signature anti-guarantee move: "I'm not offering you your money back, & I'll
  tell you why... That's not a guarantee. That's giving up together."
- Casual asides: "Pretty cool, right?", "Got it?", "Let me flip it."
- His own thesis, already a video title: **"streams aren't important."**

## Never
Hype adjectives, guru language, "unlock / leverage / supercharge", income
claims, exclamation stacking, fake urgency.

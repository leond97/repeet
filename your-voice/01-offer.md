# repeet — the offer

Done-for-you Meta ad campaigns for Spotify songs and releases.
**Separate brand from Launch.Lab** (which sells track finishing / coaching to
producers). Never mix the two.

- **Price:** $400 per release (agency fee) + **$500 minimum ad spend**, paid by
  the artist from their own ad account. repeet never touches the ad budget.
- **Capacity:** 5 artists a month, every campaign run by hand.
- **Lead time:** 2 weeks before release day.
- **Destination:** https://repeetagency.com — an **application form**, not a
  checkout. Leon reads every application and answers by email within 24h.
- **Voice:** repeet writes as "we", never "I". Founded by Leon D.

## The mechanism (what makes it different)
Campaigns are optimised for **saves, follows and playlist adds**, not clicks or
raw streams. Those are the signals Spotify's algorithm reads, so momentum
survives after the budget stops. Cheap clicks buy a listener who taps and
leaves — a number that teaches Spotify nothing.

## What is NOT promised
No guaranteed stream count. Ever. The angle: a guaranteed number has to be
delivered, and when ads alone don't reach it, bots are the only way left —
which gets releases flagged and removed. The only commitment is **3+ streams
per dollar of ad spend** and **100% real, verifiable listeners**.

## Ideal client
Artists and producers who release regularly, have a finished mastered track,
and can put $500+ behind a campaign. Most have already tried Meta ads
themselves and got a spike that died.

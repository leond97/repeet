## AD 1 — "Check it yourself" · numbered-listicle
**Drive/belief:** safety — *"everyone is a scam and I can't tell the difference."*
**Wall removed:** they can't verify anyone, so they buy nothing.
**Opens in:** problem-aware.

### Primary text
Before you pay anyone to promote your song, open Spotify for Artists and check three numbers. It takes about 30 seconds, and it tells you more than any review page will.

**1. Streams per listener.** Real people come back. If a campaign delivered 20,000 streams and nearly 20,000 unique listeners, nobody listened twice. That isn't an audience. That's traffic.

**2. Saves against plays.** Bots don't save. They don't follow you either. A stream count climbing while your saves stay flat is the clearest tell there is.

**3. Where the listeners are.** Check the cities. If your release is suddenly big in three places with no scene for it and no ad spend pointed there, something is generating it.

We're telling you this because it's the same check we'd want run on us. Every listener in a repeet campaign comes from a Meta ad shown to a real person who chose to press play — and every number sits in your dashboard, not ours.

Run it on your last campaign first. Plenty of artists find out that way.

Five artists a month. Apply at repeetagency.com
